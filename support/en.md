# MyMonth — Support

Questions, bug reports or feature ideas: write to [krasovskyi.yevhenii@gmail.com](mailto:krasovskyi.yevhenii@gmail.com). We usually reply within 2 business days.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. MyMonth works without an account or an internet connection.

### How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Your data is part of your iPhone backup. Restore an iCloud or computer backup (or use Quick Start) on the new iPhone and it comes along.

### Does MyMonth pay my bills?

No. It only tracks payments and reminds you about them inside the app; no money is moved.

### How do I delete all my data?

Settings → Data → Delete all data, or delete the app.

### Where do exchange rates come from?

From [ExchangeRate-API](https://www.exchangerate-api.com), updated once a day. Totals converted to another currency are approximate.
