# MyMonth — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

MyMonth ("the app") is developed by Yevhenii Krasovskyi Uslugi Informatyczne.

## Data we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you enter in the app — payments, amounts, categories, notes and settings — is stored only on your device.

## Network requests

To convert totals between currencies, the app downloads public exchange rates from [ExchangeRate-API](https://www.exchangerate-api.com) (open.er-api.com). This request contains no information about you or your payments. Like any web request, it reveals your device's IP address to that service, which is governed by its own privacy policy.

## Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no analytics and no advertising, does not track you and does not use the advertising identifier.

## Your data

You can delete all data at any time in Settings → Data → Delete all data. Deleting the app also deletes its data from your device. Your data is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup, which you control.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If the app starts processing personal data (for example, optional cloud sync or advertising), we will update this policy before that version is released.
